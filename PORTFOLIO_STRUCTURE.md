# Portfolio Structure

This guide maps the portfolio's four research divisions. The archive is intended to document independent inquiry, developing arguments, and intellectual growth—not to function as a résumé or a collection of assignments.

## Research divisions

1. [Projects](projects/README.md) — sustained, interdisciplinary investigations organized around a central subject or developing argument.
2. [Disciplinary Work](disciplinary-work/README.md) — foundational inquiry, readings, author studies, and conceptual notes within individual fields.
3. [Contemporary Analysis](Contemporary%20Analysis/README.md) — autonomous examinations of particular contemporary phenomena. Individual analyses retain their own conclusions and frameworks rather than being treated as steps in one predetermined argument.
4. [Interdisciplinary Synthesis](interdisciplinary-synthesis/README.md) — a space to compare and bring independent investigations into relation while preserving their differences and unresolved tensions.

## Suggested conventions

- Give each sustained project and each contemporary analysis its own directory and README.
- Use READMEs to state the subject, questions, framework, development/status, and links to working documents.
- Distinguish provisional notes from developed claims, and identify sources where possible.
- Preserve disagreements, revisions, and open questions as part of the record of intellectual development.
- Add disciplinary categories as substantive notes accumulate rather than creating empty folders for every possible field.
