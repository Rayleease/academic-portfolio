# Disciplinary Work

This division gathers foundational research within individual disciplines: reading notes, conceptual investigations, author studies, theoretical summaries, and questions that may later inform larger projects. These materials can remain exploratory and need not be forced into a project or a single synthesis.

## Areas of inquiry

Potential areas include:

- Philosophy
- Economics
- Psychology
- Psychoanalysis
- Religion and Theology
- History
- Political Theory

As material accumulates, create a directory for the relevant field and give substantive notes clear titles, dates or status where useful, and citations or reading references. Categories are organizational aids, not claims that a question belongs to only one discipline.
